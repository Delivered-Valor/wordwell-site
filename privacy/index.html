WordWell Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

WordWell is a Bible reading and study app for iPhone. It works without an account and doesn't collect anything about you.

What we collect: nothing. WordWell has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking across apps or websites. The app doesn't send your information to us or to anyone else, and it doesn't need an internet connection to work.

What stays on your phone: your reading position, settings, notes, highlights, and anything else you save in WordWell are stored only on your device. This version doesn't sync to iCloud.

Things you choose to do: if you export or back up your data, the file goes wherever you save or send it (for example, the Files app). You can password-protect backups, and they're encrypted using Apple's built-in tools. If you share something using the share sheet, it goes to the app or person you pick. If you tap a link to a publisher's website, WordWell asks first and then opens it in Safari. We never see any of this.

Third parties: WordWell includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. If you test WordWell through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. Apple's own privacy policy covers that.

Children: WordWell doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes: if this policy changes, we'll post the updated version at this address with a new date.
